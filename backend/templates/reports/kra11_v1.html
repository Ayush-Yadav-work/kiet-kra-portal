Introduction - KIET Deemed to be University is committed to providing students with opportunities for holistic development and enhancing their employability, professional competencies, interdisciplinary knowledge and lifelong learning skills.

In addition to the prescribed curriculum, the University offers Bridge Courses to provide students with exposure to emerging technologies, industry practices, professional skills, interdisciplinary learning and global learning resources.

  • These courses are designed to complement the regular curriculum and contribute to the development of graduate attributes, employability skills and overall student development

Objectives of Bridge Courses - The major objectives of the Bridge Courses are:

  1. To bridge the gap in students’ prerequisite knowledge and skills.
  2. To strengthen foundational concepts required for the regular curriculum.
  3. To bring students with diverse backgrounds to a common learning level.
  4. To facilitate a smooth transition into the academic programme.
